From Understanding to Chnage

Listening and understanding the problem of education inequality in Brazil is easy, although the difficult part is to convince people to act against this issue. To do this, there are a few things we as a community can do to work towards an overall elimination of this issue.

The First Step Towards Change

The first step towards helping this issue is to spread awareness. As an individual, it's hard to cause change when it's just one person speaking on the issue. This also doesn't force people to look into the bigger issue but instead just ignore it. Although, if a bigger group of people worked together to spread word of an issue, it would become more recognized by society. This is because a group of people can become stronger together and force their voices/message to be heard. Therefore, spreading awareness of poor education equality in Brazil can be the first step towards creating a solution if our community worked together for this message to be spread for people to hear. Ways to help spread awareness include talking about education inequality through social media, schools, community events, and public campaigns. These are ways that us as a community can do to help others across the world hear and understand this problem in Brazil. This is only the first step towards helping equal education opportunities in Brazil.

UNESCO's Role in Creating Equal Education

Another way to help support Brazil is to spread more awareness of big operations that target solutions towards this inequality issue. For example, UNESCO is a big organization that strives to help promote education, science, culture, and communication around the world. Currently, UNESCO has an "Education 2030 Agenda" that aims to have inclusive, equal, and quality education for everyone by 2030. The goal for this agenda would be to help support Brazil in investing in public schools, improving literacy, supporting disadvantaged students, and provide equal learning opportunities. Therefore, more awareness of this goal can greatly help it become heard and supported to help these goals get reached by 2030.

Expanding Educational Opportunities Through Technology

Google for Education is another great agenda part of a bigger organization, which is Google. This agenda is to help areas provide technology resources to areas like Brazil that don't have specific resources for better education quality. Providing resources like computers, tablets, and school supplies can greatly benefit Brazil by giving low-income students the same educational opportunities as those who are fortunate enough to have those opportunities. Therefore, allowing equality amongst all students in Brazil. To help this agenda, people can donate used technology and school supplies. Other ways include volunteering for tutoring programs, spreading awareness, creating fundraisers, and promoting projects like these.

Together, We Can Make a Difference

Overall, there are many ways to help support equal education in Brazil. There are many ways you can help weather it through awareness, donation, volunteering, or helping fundraisers/projects. Either way, these things don't take a lot of time or energy and can be done. If everyone helped to pitch in, then education equality will and is very possible to foresee for these children in Brazil. Therefore, please take some time out of your day and please help us reach our goal for these children in Brazil see a brighter feature for equal opportunities for all!
