Take Action: Summary & Resources for Educational Equality in Brazil

Education inequality in Brazil is a deep rooted issue, that affects millions of students who lack access to proper school supplies, technology, and quality instruction. However, as we have explored throughout this site, meaningful change starts with informed action and empathy. Whether through financial donations, donating unused technology, or sharing data to raise awareness, every single person can play a role in helping Brazilian students stay in school and contribute to them reaching their full potential. Below are vetted organizations, practical ways to get involved, and additional resources to continue learning.

Organizations Making a Difference (Where to Donate)

BrazilFoundation (Education Fund)

The BrazilFoundation mobilizes resources to support community-led grassroots organizations throughout Brazil. Their Education Fund is specifically focused on improving teaching-learning processes, enhancing access to early childhood education and promoting inclusive education for children in rural and urban settings.

Support the BrazilFoundation Education Fund

Instituto Ayrton Senna

Founded in 1994, this Brazilian non-profit works directly with public school systems across the country to train teachers, improve literacy rates, and develop innovative educational programs that help low-income students succeed.

Explore Instituto Ayrton Senna

Todos Pela Educação (All for Education)

An independent, non-partisan organization dedicated to ensuring quality basic public education for every child and young person in Brazil by advocating for effective public policies and holding leaders accountable.

Learn More at Todos Pela Educação

UNICEF Global Education Support

UNICEF works globally to reach out-of-school children and prevent high school dropouts, while also providing learning materials to vulnerable communities. Contributions help fund programs that keep disadvantaged youth enrolled in school and continuously learning.

Donate to UNICEF Education Programs

Other Ways You Can Help (No Donation Required)

  • Donate Used Technology: Clean out old laptops, tablets, and smartphones and donate them to reputable tech-recycling charities or school-supply drives. As highlighted on our Solutions page, bridging the digital divide is one of the fastest ways to improve learning quality for low-income students.
  • Volunteer Your Language Skills: If you speak Portuguese and English, look into virtual volunteering platforms like Translators without Borders or UN Volunteers to help translate open-source educational materials and lesson plans.
  • Amplify the Data: Share statistics from our Statistics & Evidence page on social media using hashtags like #EducationForAll and #SDG4 to bring attention to high school dropout rates and educational disparities in Brazil.
  • Organize a Local Drive: Partner with your school, club, or community center to host a fundraiser or book drive benefiting international education funds.
