About Our Team

Working Together for Equal Educational Opportunities

Who We Are

We are a team of five members: Alasia, Brittney, Nicholas, Cole, and Johnathan. We are dedicated to raising awareness about educational inequality in Brazil and promoting equal opportunities for all children. Through this website, we hope to share information about education inequality and encourage others to learn about ways they can help.

Our Mission

Our mission is to raise awareness about educational inequality in Brazil and encourage equal access to quality education. We believe every child deserves the opportunity to learn, grow, and build a better future.

Our Goals

Our goals are to educate others about educational inequality, share possible solutions, promote awareness, and encourage individuals and communities to support equal educational opportunities.

Why Education Equality Matters

Education provides children with opportunities to develop knowledge, skills, and confidence. However, not every child has equal access to educational resources and opportunities. By understanding the challenges students face in Brazil, we can help spread awareness and encourage positive change.
